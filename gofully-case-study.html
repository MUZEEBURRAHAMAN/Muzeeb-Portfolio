01Overview · What is GoFully

A capable tool that looked like every other screenshot extension

GoFully is a Chrome extension for full-page screenshots. It scrolls a page, waits for lazy images, handles sticky headers and stitches one clean image. It reads text out of any capture in six languages, marks up and redacts, beautifies captures for posts and slides, and exports PNG, JPG, WebP or PDF.

Everything runs inside the browser. There is no account and no server. Capture, editing, text reading and redaction all happen on the device, which is rare for this category of tool.

The redesign brief was to keep every feature and change how it feels. The product had grown feature by feature, and the extension, the store listing and the website showed it. The goal was one calm, consistent product whose strongest idea, privacy, comes first.

02The problem · The old GoFully

Every feature, all at once, on every surface

The extension worked well. What it said about itself, in the store, on the website and in its own UI, made it hard to see why.

A headline made of features

  • The old hero read Full Page Screenshot Tool. One Extension. Every Job.
  • The paragraph under it named WebAssembly OCR, 4K export and PDF in one sentence.
  • The privacy promise sat in a small badge above it all.

A generic identity

  • The icon was a glossy camera with flash rays.
  • In the Chrome Web Store, it sat next to other screenshot extensions using camera icons too.
  • Nothing in the mark said what made GoFully different.

Busy visuals that hid the UI

  • Store screenshots and site mockups layered gradients, tilted devices, arrows and handwritten callouts.
  • The product UI itself was small inside each picture.
  • Each surface used its own style, so the extension, store and site did not read as one product.
Where it broke downGoFully was private, precise and free. The way it looked said "another screenshot tool". The redesign had to make the product's real strengths visible on every surface, starting with the brand.

03Direction · Positioning and values

One promise for every screen: capture the whole page, keep every pixel private

Before drawing anything, I wrote down who GoFully is for and the problem it solves. The audience is everyday sharers: developers, QA testers, writers, researchers and privacy teams who send screenshots every day. The problem fits in three words from the brand guide: three tools, one leak. Long pages get cut off, private data slips through, and a capture needs three apps before it can be shared.

I then collected references for each surface (landing pages, extension popups and in-page UI, editors, components and store screenshots) and kept them as an internal moodboard, filtered for fit with a calm sky-and-navy direction.

Four values that settle every design and writing choice

When two options look equal, the guide says to pick the one that serves these better.

Private

Everything runs on the device. No account, no upload, no tracking.

Precise

Pixel-exact captures, checked seams and crops that keep the marks.

Fast

One click or one shortcut from page to finished file.

Friendly

Plain words, soft shapes and a clear next step on every screen.

04Brand · Mark, voice, type and colour

From a glossy camera to a viewfinder drawn as a G

The new symbol is a camera viewfinder drawn as the letter G. It still says "screenshot", and it can only be GoFully. Each of its four parts has a job, so the guide asks to keep every part whenever the mark is used.

Four parts, each with a job

Viewfinder frameThe rounded square of a camera screen. It holds the mark together at every size.
Lens ringFocus. The ring pulls the eye to the centre, where the capture happens.
Shutter dotThe moment of capture. Kept round, solid and off-centre.
TailCloses the shape into a G. Never trimmed or straightened.

A voice that says what happened, and what to do next

The voice is a calm expert: plain words, real numbers, no hype and no exclamation marks.

Do
Don't
"Screenshot saved. 4,200 px captured."
"Your amazing screenshot has been successfully saved!"
Toasts state the result in a few words, and errors say what to do next: Copy failed. Try Save PNG.

Colour with a tested ground for every text pair

Sky Blue #6AB0FF and Deep Navy #014073 lead the palette. Archivo carries headings and Inter carries everything people read and click. Every text colour was checked against its background, which set one hard rule: Sky is a fill and an accent, never text on white. Dark mode keeps the navy family instead of plain black, so GoFully still feels like GoFully at night.

05Design system · Foundations and components

Soft corners, a 4 px grid and two quiet shadows

The values live as variables in the Figma file, so design and code read from the same source. Navy does the work, and Sky shows focus and selection.

Radius by role

6 px inputs, 10 px buttons, 16 px cards and 24 px dialogs. Toggles and tags use a full pill.

Two shadows, tinted navy

Raised for cards and the result bar, Overlay for menus, dialogs and toasts. Never pure black.

One icon weight

A 24 px grid, 1.75 px stroke, round caps and joins. No filled icons except state dots.

Shapes from the symbol

Rings, viewfinder corners and a label chip, used one at a time, never all three together.

06Extension · The popup

One primary action per screen, and a clear way out of every error

Every capture starts in the popup. It lists each way to capture with its shortcut: full page, visible area, a selected area, a panel that scrolls, one element, draw on page and text capture.

The redesign then followed a capture through every state it can reach, including the ones that go wrong: a page Chrome won't allow, a capture that stopped halfway, and a history search with no results. Each one explains what happened and offers the next step.

07Extension · On-page overlays

Overlays that sit on any website without fighting it

Most capture modes happen on the page itself. People drag a region, hover to pick one element, or pick a panel that scrolls inside its own box. After a capture, a result card offers copy, save, edit and pin. Text capture hands back text to paste, draw on page marks up a live site before it is captured, and a pinned capture floats over any page as a reference.

08Extension · The editor

Everything after the capture, in one quiet window

The editor holds crop, arrows and shapes, text, blur, auto-redact, Beautify and export. Auto-redact scans a capture for details like emails and card numbers and covers them before anything is shared. Beautify adds a background, padding, a browser frame or a 3D tilt for posts and slides. Light and dark share one layout; drag the slider to compare them.

09Extension · Settings, guides and help

The quiet screens got the same care

Settings, step-by-step guides, a welcome page that teaches the first capture, and help articles. Guides collect a series of captures with a line under each and export one PDF or Markdown file, which turns a set of screenshots into onboarding docs or a bug report.

10Website · Old and new

From a list of features to one sentence anyone can repeat

Old: Full Page Screenshot Tool. One Extension. Every Job. New: Screenshot the whole page. Share it ready to go. The privacy promise moved from a badge into the first paragraph: all on your computer, with no account.

I tried three hero layouts against a realistic long article, then storyboarded every feature demo frame by frame, so the site shows the real extension working on a real page instead of tilted mockups.

11Where it stands · And what's next

One product, one look, one promise on every surface

Designed: the new brand and its 41-page guide, Design System v2, more than 70 extension screens and states in light and dark, and the new website.

Built: the new website runs in development and is not live yet; the live site and store listing still show the old design. The extension's current release is v1.1.3.

Next: v1.2 pushes further on privacy and precision

Redact page

Covers emails, card numbers, phone numbers, ID numbers and API keys on the live page, before the capture.

Measure

Hover any element for its size, padding, margin and text style, measure gaps, and copy any colour as hex.

Delayed capture

QR codes and barcodes

Read on the device, with a safe Open button for web links.

The features didn't change. What changed is that every surface, from a 16 px toolbar icon to the landing page, now says the same thing: capture the whole page, keep every pixel private.
