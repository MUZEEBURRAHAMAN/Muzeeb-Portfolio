04 / Methodology

How I Work

I start with questions, not screens. Before jumping into Figma, I align on first principles:

01 What’s the core problem?
02 Who experiences it and where?
03 Why does it matter to the business?

From there, I map journeys, prototype solutions, test assumptions, and collaborate tightly with engineering to ship.

I stay close to implementation — building prototypes, plugins, AI workflows, and front-end components myself.
