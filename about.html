It's Muzeeb —
you say it "muh-zeeb".

Product designer & builder for AI & SaaS — I design the system, then ship it in code.

I'm an end-to-end Product Designer & Builder with 4.5+ years of experience across AI, SaaS, and e-commerce products. My edge is that I don't stop at mockups — I build.

I've worked on websites, SaaS platforms, mobile apps, dashboards, and design systems. I usually sit somewhere between UX logic and UI craft — figuring out how things should work, what's making them confusing, and how to turn that into something clear and usable.

In my current role

At Omnis AI I lead product design across 8+ AI-powered legal products, built and scaled a 200+ component design system adopted across the suite, and turned complex legal workflows into intuitive experiences. I also built AI design-automation tools — a UX Audit Assistant and an AI Edge Fixer — that streamlined reviews and held quality at scale.

My process

usually starts with asking a lot of questions and understanding what's actually causing the problem before jumping into screens. I like mapping how things connect, finding where the flow breaks, and testing the weak spots before users hit them.

When I'm not designing

you'll usually find me building side projects, planning my next trip, keeping up with tech drama, or trying a new creative hobby — not always successfully.
