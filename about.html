How I work

I start with questions, not screens. Before I open Figma I want to know what's actually broken, who it's breaking for, and what happens if we do nothing.

Then I map the flow — where things connect, where they leak, where users hit a wall — and test the weak spots before real users do.

I stay involved past handoff. Design decisions die in implementation if the designer isn't around to defend them.
